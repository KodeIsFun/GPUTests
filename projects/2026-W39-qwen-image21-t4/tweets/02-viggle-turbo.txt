Can you really generate AI images for free? Yes — on Google Colab's free GPU, and it's fast.

I tried the obvious trick first: fewer steps, faster pictures. At four steps the image came out quick — and the text in it turned to glow mush.

Then people insisted a free "turbo" add-on does it in six steps, clean. Free claims are a dime a dozen, so I made it prove itself on a free GPU.

It's legit. An image every 21.0 seconds instead of 69.1 s — and the neon sign in my test image spells "FREE GPU LAB" perfectly. AI is famously terrible at text, so that's the real test.

Three catches nobody tells you (all handled for you in the recipe):

• you need the author's custom add-on — stock settings give you mush
• the turbo file must be applied the author's way; the standard way silently throws away most of the magic
• it works even on the compressed free-tier model, not just the full-size demo

First image takes 72.1 s (one-time model load), then it's a new image every ~21 seconds. Free tier, nothing billed.

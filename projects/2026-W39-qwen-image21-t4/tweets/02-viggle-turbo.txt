Last week I tried the obvious speed trick on Qwen's 7B image model: cut the step count. At four steps the neon sign came out as glow mush — faster, sure, and text rendering died with it.

Then someone insisted their turbo LoRA does six steps, clean. Turbo-LoRA claims are a dime a dozen, so I made it prove itself on a free Colab T4.

It's legit — but only if you respect the weird parts:

• the sigma schedule needs the author's custom ComfyUI node, and CFG has to be fully off
• the LoRA can never be merged into the weights — merging silently drops most of its update; runtime hooks only
• it still holds on quantized GGUF weights, not just the full-precision demo

Same seed as every run in the repo: "FREE GPU LAB" spelled perfectly in neon. 21.0 s per warm image vs 69.1 s without the LoRA. First render pays the model load (72.1 s), then it's an image every ~21 seconds — free tier, nothing billed.

Recipe, verified notebook, API: https://github.com/KodeIsFun/run-qwen-image-on-free-colab

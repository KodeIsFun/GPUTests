Everyone's favorite free GPU (Colab's T4) runs image models ~6× slower than it can — silently. The model gets cast to fp32, and nothing tells you.

Worse: the obvious fix (--force-fp16) hard-crashes with "aimdo memory compile error". You need a second flag almost nobody documents.

I measured my way through it:

• fp32: 38 s/step → fp16: 6.2 s/step
• a full image in ~80 s, total cost $0
• "use a smaller quant for speed" does nothing — it's compute-bound (measured, not guessed)
• text rendering survives it all: it drew a neon sign that reads FREE GPU LAB, spelled right

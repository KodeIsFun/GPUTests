The face-swap LoRA everyone's downloading runs on a free Colab GPU — but not at the settings on the tin.

At the template's own resolution, one image crawls at 258.84 s/step (give up your afternoon). Shrink the two reference photos first: same swap, 96.1 s, background and neon sign untouched.

Funniest finding: turn the LoRA off and the face is arguably MORE like the target (0.867 vs 0.6399). What you actually pay for is the hair.
